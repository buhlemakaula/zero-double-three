Using the brand

  • Ink is the home ground. Bone and orange backgrounds are for accents and social posts.
  • Orange is a spice, not a sauce. One orange moment per layout is enough.
  • The orange full stop always sits at the end of the wordmark. Never recolor it separately.
  • Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the letter B.
  • The grain texture is part of the look. Use it subtly on dark surfaces.
