The Maison

A crown for the everyday — sewn in Durban since 2015.

Black Monarchy began as a single embroidery machine and a belief that streetwear could carry itself like couture. A decade on, every set, polo and cardigan still leaves our Durban Central studio with the same red seal stitched by hand.

We don't chase the drop calendar. We make considered pieces, in modest runs, for people who'd rather own less and wear it longer.
