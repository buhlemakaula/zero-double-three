Made in Durban

Black Monarchy started with one embroidery machine and a simple idea — that streetwear could carry itself like couture without the couture price. Ten years on, every set, polo and cardigan still leaves our Durban Central studio with the red seal stitched in.

We keep it simple: considered pieces, modest runs, made for people who'd rather own less and wear it longer.
