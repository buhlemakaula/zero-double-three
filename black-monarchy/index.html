The Maison

Founded in 2015.
Sewn in Durban.

Black Monarchy began in a small studio in Durban Central with one belief — that dignity is a garment, and everyone deserves to wear it well. A decade on, every piece still leaves our hands the same way: cut, stitched and sealed with the crest.

We call our people the Court. Not customers — a lineage of self-made royalty across KwaZulu-Natal and beyond.
