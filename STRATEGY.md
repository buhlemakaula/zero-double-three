# Connect — Competitive Strategy

Research-backed positioning for the Durban on-demand market. This is the thinking that shaped the
product build.

## The incumbents

### Checkers Sixty60 — the giant
- **Strengths:** ~80%+ share of SA on-demand grocery; 47.7% YoY growth to ~R18.9bn (FY-Jun'25);
  94% on-time, ~97% fulfilment; 5.2M+ downloads; fierce customer loyalty; vibrant social /
  influencer marketing; ~60-min door delivery.
- **Weaknesses:** single-retailer (Checkers only) — **not a marketplace**; door-delivery only;
  affluent-suburb density bias; no curbside lane.

### Uber Eats — the platform
- **Strengths:** logistics density amortised across restaurants; variety (groceries, pharmacy, pet,
  alcohol, electronics); SPAR partnership for national reach; live order editing + backup-item
  selection; weekly essentials discounts; ~R17bn SA economic impact.
- **Weaknesses:** high merchant commissions; restaurant-first UX; door-delivery only; brand isn't
  "premium".

### Zulzi — the operator
- **Strengths:** dark-store rapid commerce, ~17-min average; groceries + liquor + pharmacy;
  **script upload with real-time medical-aid claims**; built the original Sixty60 platform; 7–15%
  merchant commission; CPG-funded coupons/ads.
- **Weaknesses:** lower consumer brand awareness; capex-heavy dark-store model; door-delivery only.

## The gap Connect owns

Every incumbent races to your **front door**. None own the **kerb.** Curbside collection is the
under-served lane: order on the commute, pull in, boot loaded in <3 min — no parking, no queue, no
carrying. It is faster and cheaper to operate (no last-mile driver) and perfect for Durban's
mall-and-car culture (Gateway, Ballito Junction, uMhlanga).

## How Connect wins

| Vector | Incumbent norm | Connect's move |
|---|---|---|
| **Fulfilment** | Door delivery | **Curbside boot-drop** as the hero lane + delivery + collect |
| **Assortment** | Single retailer (Sixty60) | True **marketplace**: groceries + cellar + pharmacy, one bag |
| **Payments** | Card / limited | **Every SA structure**: card, tap, Instant EFT, wallet, split-later, cash |
| **Pharmacy** | Rare | **Script upload + medical-aid claim** (matches Zulzi's best feature) |
| **Loyalty** | Points / tips | **Connect Wallet, 3% back**, every order |
| **Brand** | Mass-market | **Luxury, airy, editorial** — a premium feel rivals lack |
| **Hours** | Store-bound | Long **06:00–01:00** window covering late-night cellar & pharmacy runs |

## Why traders join (the supply side)

Marketplaces live or die on merchant supply. Connect's pitch:
1. **Keep more** — transparent flat commission below incumbent norms; no launch listing fee.
2. **Own the data** — live sales, repeat-customer insight, demand heat-maps returned to the trader.
3. **Get discovered** — editorial placement, launch-partner spotlights, CPG-funded promotions.

**21 Ridges** is the reference launch partner embedded in the app — proof of the full flow from menu
to curbside handover.

## Go-to-market (Durban-first)

1. **Beachhead:** uMhlanga · Umdloti · Ballito · Berea — car-dense, high-LSM, mall-anchored.
2. **Curbside partnerships:** mall bays & retail forecourts as branded Connect collection points.
3. **Anchor merchants:** one hero per vertical (21 Ridges → Harbour & Vine → The Ridge Cellar →
   Meridian Pharmacy), then widen.
4. **Compliance as trust:** ID-at-boot for liquor, pharmacist-verified scripts — a premium,
   responsible brand.

## Guardrails

- Liquor **18+** ID verification at handover; no sales outside trading hours.
- Pharmacy scripts pharmacist-verified; cold-chain items in insulated curbside pouches.
- POPIA-aligned data handling.
