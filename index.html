Good to know

Questions, answered

How does the Block Pass actually work?

You pay one monthly amount and your perks load onto your account. Show your name or number at any of the three doors — coffee, laundry loads and sneaker cleans come off your balance, and everything else gets your member discount.

Is paying online safe?

Yes. Payments run through Paystack, a licensed payment provider used across South Africa. Your card details go directly to Paystack — this site never sees or stores them.

How long does a sneaker clean take?

Deep cleanses take 48–72 hours, refurbishments a little longer. You get an SMS the moment they're ready — no need to phone and check.

Where do you pick up and deliver?

Across the Berea: Musgrave, Essenwood, Glenwood, Morningside, Windermere, Overport, Umbilo, Bulwer and Sydenham. Somewhere else? WhatsApp us — if we can reach you, we will.

Can I cancel my Block Pass?

Anytime, no notice period. Your perks run to the end of the month you've paid for, and nothing bills after that.
