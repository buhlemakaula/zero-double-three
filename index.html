Your table in the city

A premium taste, savoured in the heart of Pietermaritzburg

❧

21 Ridges Bistro & Bar brings memorable flavours and carefully prepared dishes to 22 Doig Street. From slow mornings over Eggs Benedict to long evenings of grills, curries, fresh sushi and cocktails — every plate is made to be lingered over.

Our space is designed for relaxed meals, celebrations and weekend gatherings, with friendly staff ready to make every visit feel effortless. Whether it's a quiet lunch, a family feast or a night out at the bar, there's always a reason to pull up a chair.

Good to know

Frequently Asked

Where is 21 Ridges Bistro & Bar located?

We're at 22 Doig Street in the heart of Pietermaritzburg, KwaZulu-Natal — easy to find and central to the city.

How do I book a table?

Fill in the reservation form above and it sends your booking straight to our WhatsApp, or message us directly on +27 78 377 5875. We confirm within minutes during opening hours.

What is Sushi & RnB Night?

Every Thursday we host Sushi & RnB Night — fresh sushi rolls, chilled vibes and live RnB beats. It's our signature midweek night out; book ahead to secure a table.

What food do you serve?

A full bistro menu — breakfasts, burgers, grills, curries, pastas and pizzas — plus fresh hand-rolled sushi, sharing platters and a premium bar of cognac, whisky, gin, champagne and cocktails.

Are you good for groups and celebrations?

Absolutely. Our space is built for relaxed meals, celebrations and weekend gatherings, with sharing platters and a full bar. WhatsApp us ahead of time for larger groups.
