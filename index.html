01/ About us

We create furniture with thoughtful architecture, clean lines and attention to detail — for those who choose not just beautiful objects, but a quality of life. Misitu means forests in Swahili, and every piece starts there: responsibly sourced kiaat, blackwood, walnut and yellowwood.

We believe all furniture should feel good, look strong, and last beyond trends — built by hand in South Africa and delivered to your door.

02/ How we work

Selected timber

Every board is hand-graded for grain and stability, then kiln-dried in our yard before a single cut is made.

Traditional joinery

Dovetails, mortise-and-tenon and hand-fitted joints — no staples, no shortcuts, no visible fixings.

Finished by hand

Three coats of natural hard-wax oil, rubbed back between each — a finish you can repair, not replace.
