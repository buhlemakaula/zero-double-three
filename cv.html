01Profile

Five years in printing, signage and branding. I design it and I produce it.

Graphic designer and DTP operator with over five years in the printing, signage and branding industry. I build logos, banners, signage, mockups and marketing material, then take them through production: heat pressing, dye sublimation and large format printing.

Most of that work happens in fast production environments where accuracy and speed matter as much as the idea. I am currently expanding the technical side with a National Diploma in IT Systems Development.
