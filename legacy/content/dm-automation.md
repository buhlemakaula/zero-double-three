# DM Automation Playbook (the Jens Heitmann funnel, our version)

The reference screenshot is a ManyChat style flow: someone follows or comments, an automatic DM offers a freebie, a button delivers it. It works because the freebie is genuinely useful and the ask is tiny. Here is our version, built on assets that already exist.

## The funnel

Follow or comment trigger -> auto DM with a yes button -> deliver the free AI Starter Kit -> soft pitch (daily posts + WhatsApp booking). The comment triggers are already printed on our carousels: SYSTEM, BRAND, CV, LINK, AGENT.

## Setup (once, about an hour)

1. Instagram must be a Professional account (already in the profile kit launch order).
2. Create a free ManyChat account (manychat.com), the official Meta partner most creators use. Connect it to the Instagram account. The free tier covers up to 1000 contacts, which is plenty to start.
3. Build two automations with the scripts below: the comment keyword flow and the welcome DM flow.
4. Keep Meta's rules: automated DMs only to people who acted first (commented, messaged, or followed with welcome flow on). Never cold DM.

## Flow 1: comment keyword (main growth engine)

Trigger: user comments SYSTEM (repeat the automation per keyword: BRAND, CV, LINK, AGENT).

- **Public auto reply (ManyChat posts under their comment):** Sent you a DM!
- **DM 1:** Hey, Buhle here. You asked for the system breakdown, so here it is. Quick question first so Instagram lets this thread stay open: want it now? [Button: Yes please]
- **DM 2 (on button tap):** Here you go: my free AI Starter Kit. The prompt formula I use daily, five prompts to steal, and the honesty test almost nobody runs. [Button: Get the kit -> https://buhlemakaula.github.io/zero-double-three/assets/brand/buhle-ai-starter-kit.pdf] I post one real AI skill every day, receipts included. If you ever need design or an AI setup, my WhatsApp is on the link page.

## Flow 2: welcome DM (new followers)

- **DM 1:** Hey, thanks for the follow. As a small thank you I have a free AI Starter Kit: the exact prompt formula and five prompts I use to run my design business. Want it? [Button: Yes please]
- **DM 2:** [same delivery message as Flow 1 DM 2]

## Flow 3: story reply trigger

Every story sticker asks a question. Anyone who replies gets Flow 1 DM 1. Story replies are the strongest engagement signal Instagram tracks.

## Why this converts

- The kit is real value, not a coupon. People screenshot and share it, which loops back into growth.
- The yes button before the link doubles delivery rates (Instagram keeps threads open once the user interacts).
- Every delivery ends with the same funnel as everything else: daily posts plus WhatsApp booking.

## Later upgrades

- Swap the PDF link for an email capture page (MailerLite or Beehiiv free tier) when ready to build a newsletter like the reference account. The DM scripts stay identical, only the link changes.
- Add a WhatsApp Channel for broadcast, SA audiences live there.
