# Thumbnail Style Guide

Rules for every Pipeline Lab thumbnail.

- **Text Placement:** Bold headline stacked top-left, 1–2 lines, 2–4 words per line; key word highlighted on an accent banner. Variant: one elegant large word across the bottom. Text never overlaps the focal subject.
- **Font Style:** Heavy extra-bold uppercase sans-serif, slight italic/skew on banners; classy serif for brand-name drops. White fill, legible at small sizes.
- **Color Scheme:** Three-color system — near-black background, white text, one signature accent color for banners, glowing 3D icons, graph lines, and ambient rim glow. (Reference uses orange; The Pipeline Lab swaps in the emerald→blue gradient.)
- **Subject Positioning:** Focal subject on the right third with authority energy. Faceless adaptation: a consistent branded focal object — the glowing flask icon or a 3D mascot — holds this position with a gradient halo.
- **Background Composition:** Clean dark canvas with subtle grid texture; glossy 3D app icons, "+" combining symbols, dashed connector lines linking mascots into system diagrams, rising graph lines. Visualizes "systems and money going up" at a glance.
- **Emotional Hook:** Opportunity + authority. Formula: big money promise or system reveal (text/diagram) + authoritative focal subject = "this channel figured it out, and I'm missing out." Icon equations (X + Y = $) drive curiosity.
